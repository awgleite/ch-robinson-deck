Prototype caveats

What was built for this conversation, and where it stops.

  • The write-back POST targets a test endpoint, not Navisphere. What's proven is the payload shape and the delivery, not the connection.
  • The arrival timestamp is seeded data. The geofence mechanism is real; that specific number did not come off a truck.
  • Routing is free-flow road distance on a car profile, not live traffic, and not a 53-foot profile. The production equivalent is a commercial truck router.
  • Detention and TONU values are market ranges, not contract rates.
  • Hours-of-Service rules are industry knowledge from memory. They were not verified against the regulator for this exercise.
  • The dashboard dataset is synthetic, except the record generated by the recorded call.
  • The two-hour free time and the release trigger are conditions of this scenario, not asserted market practice.
